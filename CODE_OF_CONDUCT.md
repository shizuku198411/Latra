# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus technical discussions on evidence and the work itself.
- Welcome questions and differing perspectives.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with empathy.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable in any project space.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through an available private repository contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible. Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of reporters to the extent reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
